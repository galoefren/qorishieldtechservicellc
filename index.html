About us

Privacy-first by design

Many employers installed fingerprint time clocks years ago and never checked them against biometric privacy rules on notice, written consent, retention and destruction. Most can't answer a simple question: who accessed our employees' biometric data last month, and why?

Qori ShieldTech exists to answer that question, with clear reports for leadership, HR and IT.

No templates stored. We keep access metadata only, never fingerprints or face templates.
Read-only access. Our connectors never change how your systems work.
People decide. AI explains and drafts; a person reviews every critical alert.
